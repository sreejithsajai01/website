Facilities

Infrastructure & Facilities

Our Facilities

We believe that a child's environment plays a vital role in shaping their learning journey. Hence we have thoughtfully designed our campus to provide a safe, stimulating, and future-ready learning space that supports both academic excellence and holistic development.

Smart Classrooms

Our classrooms are spacious, well-ventilated and equipped with modern digital teaching tools that make learning interactive, engaging, and easy to understand.

Early Childhood Learning Spaces

We have specially designed learning areas for Nursery, LKG and UKG focus on activity-based learning, creativity and exploration.

Digital Learning & Computer Education

Students are introduced to technology from an early age through guided computer education and digital learning resources.

Creative Arts & Activity Programs

Students participate in a variety of co-curricular activities including art, music, dance, storytelling and creative expression.

Safe & Secure Campus

We prioritize the safety of our students and thus our campus is monitored and maintained with strict safety measures.

Sports & Play Areas

Our well-maintained indoor and outdoor play spaces encourage physical activity, teamwork and healthy development.

Clean & Hygienic Environment

We maintain high standards of cleanliness and hygiene across the campus to ensure the well-being of every child.

Parent-School Communication

In the spirit of partnership with parents, our communication channels ensure that they stay informed about their child's progress.

At St. Mary's CBSE School, every facility is designed with one goal in mind — to create an environment where children can learn with curiosity, grow with confidence, and move from darkness to light through knowledge and values.

St. Mary's CBSE School

St. Mary's CBSE School Vashi is run by The Malankara Orthodox Church Council of Bombay (MOCCB) which manages and operates the St. Mary's Group of Institutions in various parts of Maharashtra & Gujarat. We are committed to providing quality education and nurturing the overall development of our students.
