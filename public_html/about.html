About St. Mary's CBSE School

Spreading Light Through Quality Education

Our History

St. Mary's Multipurpose High School & Junior College, Vashi, an institution established in 1986, has been a beacon of quality education and holistic development since its inception. Guided by the inspiring motto "Lead Us from Darkness to Light", the institution has been committed to nurturing young minds and shaping a brighter future for its students for nearly four decades.

Through this journey of academic excellence, we have been witnesses of the evolving demographics of Vashi. Amidst this rapid change we have identified the rising need for diverse streams of education – leading us to embark on yet another journey. And hence, St. Mary's CBSE School - a newly established sister institution committed to shaping confident, curious, and compassionate learners for the future.

Our motto for this new institution is "Spreading Light" - reflecting our belief that education illuminates the path of growth and success. At St. Mary's, we guide children towards wisdom, character, and purpose through empowering their young minds with knowledge and by helping them to grow into responsible individuals who can think critically, act ethically, and thus be an asset to the society.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

We envision a learning community where every child discovers their unique potential and grows into a responsible and future-ready individual.

Our Mission

To nurture confident, compassionate and competent learners by providing a strong academic foundation rooted in integrity, discipline and moral excellence.

  • Spread the light of knowledge through quality education
  • Spread the light of values by nurturing honesty and respect
  • Spread the light of confidence by encouraging independent thinking
  • Spread the light of creativity through joyful learning
  • Spread the light of innovation through modern teaching methods

St. Mary's CBSE School

St. Mary's CBSE School Vashi is run by The Malankara Orthodox Church Council of Bombay (MOCCB) which manages and operates the St. Mary's Group of Institutions in various parts of Maharashtra & Gujarat. We are committed to providing quality education and nurturing the overall development of our students.
